Mock-up of a 3D screen for the office board. The county terrain, lakes and imagery are real. Barrie, Midhurst, Shanty Bay, Alcona and Angus have their real buildings in 3D, about 54,000 of them, each one's height measured from Ontario's lidar, with roofs and streets from the provincial aerial photos. The sharp photo and buildings load in as the camera gets close. The trucks are 3D models of the Hino 195 and Hino L7 on sample jobs, driving real roads about 8× faster than real life and drawn larger than life so they read from across the office. Drag the map to look around; the tour picks back up after a few seconds.

County imagery: Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 by EOX, contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data · Terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen) · Barrie-area photos and lidar: Ontario Imagery Web Map Service (SCOOP), Ontario Digital Surface and Terrain Models, contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Ontario · Roads and building outlines: Overture Maps, © OpenStreetMap contributors
